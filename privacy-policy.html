Introduction

Welcome to our Privacy Policy. This document explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our application.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

1. Information You Provide

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, and contact details you provide to us.

2. Automatically Collected Information

  • Usage Data: We collect data about how you interact with the app, such as feature usage and app performance, to improve our services.

3. Permission-Based Data

  • Notifications: To deliver reminders of when games become available for your convenience, we require permission to send push notifications.

We cannot guarantee its absolute security.
